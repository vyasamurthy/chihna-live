THE SECOND HALF

What agents did, and what they withstood.

Execution receipts, signed receipts and compliance receipts all record the runtime half — actions taken. That half is being standardised quickly and well. The withstood half — the adversarial validation evidence, what a system survived and what honestly happened — has no carrier.

The validation receipt is that carrier. It composes with the receipts wave rather than competing with it: the runtime half is theirs, the withstood half is this profile, and the ledger head is the join point. Together they are the full chihna.

THE NAME

The mark from which the claim is inferred.

In Nyaya logic a chihna is the evidentiary mark from which a claim is inferred — the smoke from which fire is known. That is the thesis in one word: evidence is a mark, not an assertion; verdicts are inferred from marks, never trusted from vendors.

Chihna is the proof layer. AVH is the engine that produces it — the adversarial validation harness. Each published artifact is a chihna: a portable proof object of campaign, ledger and attestations.

THE HONEST-STATISTICS RULE

Non-conformant by format, not discouraged by policy.

The industry reports pass rates and the industry hides inconclusives. A rule that depends on the issuer's good manners is not a rule, so this one is carried by the receipt format itself and checked by the verifier.

  • Inconclusive is a normative field. A receipt omitting cases_inconclusive fails validation. There is no conformant way to publish a campaign without saying how often the answer was unclear.
  • Counts must sum. passed + failed + inconclusive + not_sent = total, checked arithmetically. A receipt whose numbers do not reconcile is non-conformant.
  • Inconclusive is not pass. It is reported in the receipt, in the report, and on this site, everywhere the campaign appears.
  • Reliability needs an interval, not a streak. A reliable-pass requires the Wilson 95% lower bound above threshold, so five passes out of five cannot claim ninety percent.

Of the 608 cases published here, 210 are inconclusive — roughly a third. A deterministic analyser routinely produces them, and a conservative judge leaves genuinely ambiguous responses unresolved by design. Hiding them is the exact failure mode this format makes impossible.

ARCHITECTURE

Four layers, and the one you can check from a browser.

Each layer below is mapped to shipped modules. The receipt layer is what travels; the relying-party layer is who checks.

L1 · EVIDENCE

What happened

Append-only case log, evidence pack with verdict counts and Wilson intervals, hash-chained behaviour ledger, append-only budget ledger. Built for tamper-evidence before readability.

L2 · PROOF

What binds

Evaluation attestation binds the campaign to its pack by SHA-256; continuity attestation binds the pack to the ledger head. Acceptance gates are evaluated against the pack, and forcing one attaches the failed gates to the record.

L3 · RECEIPT

What travels

The validation receipt, profile 0.1 — one JSON object a relying party checks without the artifacts. Subject, honest counts, evidence digests, ledger head, and the issuer's own verification result, labelled as a claim.

L4 · RELYING PARTY

Who checks

Two keyless tiers. The receipt tier runs here in your browser in seconds. The evidence tier re-derives everything from the artifacts with one command in the engine — log replay, recomputed hashes, ledger walk.

PROOF CHAIN · EACH LINK BOUND TO THE LAST BY DIGEST

No keys, no network, no trusted third party. Anyone can verify a validation receipt with a JSON parser and a hash function. The seller of the evidence is not the validator of the evidence — that separation is the product.

ECONOMICS

Evidence at commodity cost, under a hard cap.

The entire published campaign history — sixteen campaigns, 608 cases against frontier models — cost $1.37. The spend cap is checked before every target call and enforced portfolio-wide; the ledger is append-only and must reconcile with the pack's own claims, or verification fails them apart.

The discipline is the cap's existence rather than its number. It was raised from $100 to $150 on 3 November 2026 as headroom, not as a spend plan.

AUTHORISATION

Nothing is interpreted. Nothing is sent unauthorised.

A deterministic Scope Guard maps every case to an authorisation basis — your own deployment, standard API terms, or a registered programme — and refuses the rest before a single request leaves. The frontier adapter adds a second, independent gate.

Receipts name their subject. Mock and free-tier targets must be identified as such, so a receipt cannot imply frontier-class testing that did not happen.
