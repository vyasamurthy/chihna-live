READING THE TABLE

Why a third of the cases are inconclusive.

Roughly a third of all published cases resolved to neither pass nor fail. The dominant population is judge-unclear: leak probes answered with truthful deflections, and benign-appearing compliance. The conservative judge leaves both unresolved by design, because a judge that resolves ambiguity in its own favour is not a judge.

Smaller populations are hedged compliance — engaged and disclaimed in the same response — and empty responses, where there is simply nothing to judge.

WHY SOME CAMPAIGNS REPEAT A MODEL

Re-runs are published, not substituted.

Several subjects appear more than once. Published packs are immutable because receipts attest them, so when the analyser improves, the honest fix is not to mutate an old pack — it is to re-run the campaign live and publish a new chihna alongside the old one.

Both rows stay in the table. The earlier campaign is not deleted and its inconclusive count is not quietly revised.

THE STANDARDS DONATION

COMPOSITION

The join with the receipts wave

The runtime half is deliberately replaceable. Execution, signed and compliance receipts can carry the actions; runtime.ledger_head_sha256 is the join point to the withstood half.

Every receipt linked above contains digests and counts only — no prompts, no responses, no keys, no credentials. The evidence artifacts stay with the issuer; the receipt is what travels.
